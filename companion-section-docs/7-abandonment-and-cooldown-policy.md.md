# ABANDONMENT & COOLDOWN POLICY

This policy governs customer behavior during RNG Companion booking.

Its purpose is to protect companion time, maintain marketplace liquidity, and prevent system abuse.

---

## 1. What Is Abandonment?

An abandonment event occurs when a customer:

- Starts an RNG search and:
  - Leaves the page
  - Closes the tab
  - Does not select a companion within the decision timer
  - Uses all rerolls without selecting a companion

Abandonment does NOT occur if:
- No companions were found during eligibility check
- RNG was unavailable due to low supply

---

## 2. Lock Timers (RNG Companion)

Initial search window:
- 60 seconds

Reroll windows:
- 30 seconds each (maximum 2 rerolls)

Maximum lock duration per companion:
- Non-locked companions → 60 seconds
- Locked companions → 120 seconds (60 + 30 + 30)

All companions are automatically released when:
- A selection is made
- The timer expires
- The customer abandons

---

## 3. Abandonment Counting

Each abandonment event increments a daily counter for the customer.

The counter resets every 24 hours.

---

## 4. Cooldown Rules

To prevent abuse:

- 2 abandonments within 24 hours → 5-minute RNG cooldown
- 5 abandonments within 24 hours → 24-hour RNG restriction
- Repeated abuse over multiple days → deposit required for future RNG access (admin-enforced)

Cooldown affects:
- RNG Companion booking only
- Manual browsing remains available

---

## 5. Abuse Detection

System tracks:

- RNG searches started
- RNG searches completed
- Selection rate
- Abandonment rate

Customers with abnormal patterns may be:

- Temporarily restricted
- Required to verify payment method
- Required to place refundable deposit before RNG access

Admin has full visibility into abandonment metrics.

---

## 6. Companion Protection

Companions:

- Are automatically released after timer expiration
- Cannot be locked beyond defined limits
- Are not informed of abandonment metrics to prevent psychological distortion

RNG exposure statistics are tracked internally for fairness balancing.
