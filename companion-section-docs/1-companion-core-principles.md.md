# COMPANION — CORE PRINCIPLES (HYBRID MODEL)

These principles govern all Companion workflows.

1. Companion services are service-based, not result-based.

2. Pricing is hybrid and companion-defined:
   - PER_GAME (fixed price per game)
   - TIME_BASED (platform-defined duration tiers: 15 / 30 / 60 minutes)
   - ONE_TIME (fixed price per action)

3. Matching for PER_GAME and TIME_BASED services includes a mandatory RNG system.
   - All eligible companions participate in RNG.
   - Selection is based on weighted random balancing.
   - Exposure fairness is protected.

4. The platform protects supply health:
   - Soft-reserve mechanics during RNG.
   - Lock timers are strictly limited.
   - Abandonment and cooldown policies prevent abuse.

5. Custom services are globally defined and require admin approval before becoming available.

6. Chat exists only while session is ACTIVE.

7. Payments are escrow-based and occur only after companion selection.

8. Refunds are rule-based, not emotional.

9. Tipping is allowed only after COMPLETED sessions.
   - Companion receives 90%.
   - Platform receives 10%.

10. Off-platform communication is allowed
    - Off-platform payments are strictly prohibited.
