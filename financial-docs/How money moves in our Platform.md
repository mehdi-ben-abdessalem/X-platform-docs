# 1️⃣ Big Picture: What Kind of System Are We Running?

Our platform is **not** a simple shop.

We are operating three things at the same time:

1. A **wallet system** (customers preload money as coins)
    
2. A **locked balance system** (coins get reserved and temporarily frozen for a specific order)
    
3. A **performance marketplace** (boosters earn money based on results)
    

To understand money movement, we must separate:

- 💵 Money we hold
    
- 🧾 Money we owe
    
- 💰 Money we truly earned
    

---

# 2️⃣ Step One: Customer Buys Coins

When a customer pays €100:

- We receive €100 in our bank.
    
- The customer gets €100 worth of coins in their wallet.
    

Important:

That €100 is **not our profit**.

It is money we are holding for the customer until they use it.

Think of it like a gift card.

We owe them services worth €100.

---

# 3️⃣ Step Two: Customer Creates a Boost Order

When a customer starts a boost:

- Coins are moved from their wallet
    
- Into a temporary **locked balance** (this simply means the money is reserved only for that order and cannot be used elsewhere)
    

Nothing is earned yet.

We are simply reserving the money for that order.

It still belongs to the customer until progress happens.

---

# 4️⃣ Step Three: Booster Makes Progress

This is where things get interesting.

Let’s say:

Order value = €100

If a booster improves the account by 50%, then:

- €50 is considered delivered value
    
- €50 becomes real revenue for the platform
    
- Booster earns their share of that €50
    
- €50 remains locked for future progress
    

Revenue is only created when real progress happens.

Not when payment happens.

---

# 5️⃣ If Progress Goes Backwards

Sometimes performance decreases.

Example:

- Booster reaches +50%
    
- Later session drops progress to +25%
    

Now the customer only has 25% real improvement.

This means:

- We previously counted €50 as earned
    
- But only €25 is truly delivered now
    

So we must:

- Reduce our revenue to match the new reality
    
- Return €25 back into the order’s locked balance
    
- Deduct €25 from the booster’s balance
    

This keeps our system honest.

Revenue must always equal the customer’s current permanent progress.

---

# 6️⃣ When Does Booster Get Paid?

Boosters do not get paid immediately.

There are three stages:

1. Earned (based on progress)
    
2. Held (waiting until order finishes)
    
3. Withdrawable (after order completes)
    

This protects the platform from:

- Disputes
    
- Reversals
    
- Negative sessions
    

---

# 7️⃣ What Is Actually “Our” Money?

This is very important.

At any moment, our bank balance includes:

- Customer wallet money
    
- Customer locked balance money
    
- Booster unpaid earnings
    
- Our real profit
    

Only the last one is truly ours.

Everything else belongs to someone else.

---

# 8️⃣ When Do We Make Profit?

Profit happens only when:

Customer progress increases  
AND  
That progress is permanent

Profit does **not** happen:

- When coins are purchased
    
- When an order starts
    
- When a session starts
    

Profit only happens when real value is delivered.

---

# 9️⃣ Why We Track Every € Twice (Simple Safety Rule)

We use a simple safety rule in our system:

Every time money moves, we record:

• Where it came from  
• Where it went

For example:

If customer coins increase,  
then something else must also increase (like money in the bank).

If revenue increases,  
then customer locked money must decrease.

This guarantees:

- Money cannot magically appear
    
- Money cannot disappear
    
- Every € is always traceable
    

This keeps the system stable even if:

- Stripe retries payments
    
- Sessions update multiple times
    
- Boosters go into debt
    
- Orders get cancelled
